# Omoji Pearcat

哦猫基梨 · Omoji Pearcat · オモジ梨猫

Official desktop companion downloads for macOS and Windows.

[Download the latest release](https://github.com/omojipearcat/omoji-pearcat-downloads/releases/latest) · [Official website](https://omojipearcat.com/?desktop=1)

## Version 0.1.2

- macOS Universal for Apple silicon and Intel Macs
- Windows x64
- 简体中文 · English · 日本語
- Connect your account through the official website to sync your companion's level, satiety, strawberries and jam
- Play unlocked actions, feed your companion and keep it nearby on your desktop

You can start without an account. To sync your progress, choose the account connection option in the app, sign in on the official website, compare the six-digit codes and confirm the connection. Devices can be disconnected from the website.

Each release includes a `SHA256SUMS.txt` file for verifying its installers.
